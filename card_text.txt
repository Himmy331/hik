The Changer
Link-4 / DARK / Cyberse / ATK: 2800
2+ Link monsters

If this card is Link Summoned: You can target up to 5 cards on the field and/or in either Graveyard; change their Attributes to Attributes of your choice. You can only use this effect of "The Changer" once per turn.

If you and your opponent each control a monster (except this card): You can switch control of 1 monster you control and 1 monster your opponent controls. You can only use this effect of "The Changer" once per turn.
